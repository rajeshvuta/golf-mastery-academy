Golf Rules & Etiquette

Master the rules of the game and play with confidence

The Fundamentals

The Basic Principle

Play the course as you find it: The fundamental principle of golf is to play the ball as it lies and the course as you find it. This means you cannot improve your position, except as specifically allowed by the rules.
Play by the rules: Golf is a game of honor. You are expected to call penalties on yourself, even when no one else sees the infraction.

Starting Your Round

Teeing off: The ball must be played from between the tee markers and within two club-lengths behind them. You may tee the ball up or play from the ground.
Order of play (tee box): On the first hole, determine order by drawing lots or mutual agreement. After the first hole, the player with the lowest score on the previous hole has "honors" and tees off first.
Out of bounds on tee shot: If your ball goes out of bounds (white stakes), you must replay from the tee with a one-stroke penalty. Your next shot will be your third stroke.

Playing the Ball

Play it as it lies: You must play the ball from where it comes to rest without improving its position, lie, or line of play. You cannot bend, break, or press down grass or vegetation.
Order of play (after tee): The player farthest from the hole plays first. This is called being "away." In casual play, "ready golf" is often used to speed up pace of play.
Ball moved by natural forces: If wind, water, or gravity moves your ball after it comes to rest, play it from its new position with no penalty.
Ball moved by player: If you cause your ball to move (except when making a stroke), you incur a one-stroke penalty and must replace the ball.

Penalty Situations

Out of bounds (OB): White stakes or lines mark out of bounds. Stroke and distance penalty - replay from where you last played and add one penalty stroke. If your tee shot goes OB, your next shot is your third.
Lost ball: You have 3 minutes to search for your ball. If not found, it's a lost ball. Same as out of bounds - stroke and distance penalty (one-stroke penalty, replay from original spot).
Water hazard (yellow stakes/lines): One-stroke penalty. Options: (1) Play from where you last played, (2) Drop behind the hazard keeping the point where it entered between you and the hole, or (3) Drop within two club-lengths of where it entered (no closer to hole).
Lateral water hazard (red stakes/lines): One-stroke penalty. Same options as yellow hazard, plus: (4) Drop within two club-lengths of where the ball last crossed the margin of the hazard, not nearer the hole.
Unplayable lie: You may declare any ball unplayable (except in a water hazard). One-stroke penalty. Options: (1) Stroke and distance, (2) Drop within two club-lengths not nearer the hole, or (3) Drop on a line behind the unplayable spot and the hole.

On the Green

Mark your ball: You may mark, lift, and clean your ball on the putting green. Place a coin or ball marker directly behind your ball before lifting it.
Line of putt: You cannot touch your line of putt to improve it (no pressing down spike marks or fixing imperfections). You MAY repair ball marks and remove loose impediments.
Flagstick: You may leave the flagstick in or have it removed for any putt. If the ball hits the flagstick when left in, there is no penalty. If you putt from off the green and hit the flagstick, no penalty.
Ball in motion hit by another ball: No penalty in stroke play. In match play, player whose ball was at rest may choose to replay.
Wrong green: If your ball comes to rest on another putting green, you must take relief (no penalty). Drop within one club-length of the nearest point of relief, not nearer the hole.

Bunkers

Before the stroke: You cannot touch the sand with your club before your stroke (no grounding the club or practice swings touching sand). Penalty: Two strokes or loss of hole.
Unplayable in bunker: If your ball is unplayable in a bunker, you have the standard unplayable options (one-stroke penalty), but you must drop in the bunker. Or, take a two-stroke penalty and drop outside the bunker on a line behind where the ball lay.
Raking bunkers: After playing from a bunker, you should rake the bunker to smooth the sand for following players.

Relief Situations (No Penalty)

Immovable obstruction: Man-made objects that cannot be moved (cart paths, sprinkler heads, etc.). If your ball lies on or your stance is interfered with by an immovable obstruction, you get free relief. Drop within one club-length of nearest point of relief, not nearer the hole.
Ground under repair (GUR): Areas marked with white lines or blue stakes. Free relief - drop within one club-length of nearest point of relief.
Embedded ball: If your ball is embedded in its own pitch mark in the general area (not in bunkers or penalty areas), you may take free relief by dropping within one club-length of the spot, not nearer the hole.
Movable obstruction: Man-made objects that can be moved (rakes, cans, etc.). You may remove them without penalty. If moving it causes your ball to move, replace the ball with no penalty.

Common Rule Violations

Improving lie: Cannot press down grass, break branches, or otherwise improve your lie or swing path. Penalty: Two strokes or loss of hole.
Wrong ball: If you play the wrong ball, you get a two-stroke penalty in stroke play and must correct the error. In match play, you lose the hole.
Hit ball twice: If you accidentally hit your ball twice in one stroke, count it as one stroke with no penalty (rule changed in 2019).
Ball hits you or equipment: If your ball in motion hits you, your caddie, or your equipment, one-stroke penalty.

Golf Etiquette

Pace of Play

Keep up with the group ahead: Your goal should be to keep pace with the group in front of you, not stay ahead of the group behind you. If you fall more than one hole behind, allow faster groups to play through.
Be ready to play: Be at your ball and ready when it's your turn. Select your club and read your putt while others are playing.
Limit practice swings: One or two practice swings maximum. Take them away from your ball.
Play provisional ball: If your ball might be lost or out of bounds, play a provisional ball immediately to save time.

On the Course

Silence and stillness: Do not talk, move, or stand near a player who is preparing to hit. Stand where you won't distract them.
Yell "Fore!": If your ball is heading toward another player, immediately yell "FORE!" loudly to warn them.
Replace divots: Replace and press down any divots you make with iron shots, or fill with sand/seed mix if provided.
Repair ball marks: Fix your ball marks on the green and at least one other if you see them.
Rake bunkers: After playing from a bunker, rake your footprints and the area where you played. Leave the rake outside the bunker.

On the Green

Don't step on putting lines: Be careful not to step on the line between another player's ball and the hole.
Tend the flag properly: If asked to tend the flag, stand to the side, hold it steady, and remove it before the ball reaches the hole.
Don't walk in front of putts: Walk around other players' lines, even if it takes longer.
Mark efficiently: Mark your ball quickly when asked. If your marker is on someone's line, move it one or more putter-head-lengths to the side.
Leave green promptly: Once everyone has putted out, leave the green immediately. Mark your scores at the next tee, not on the green.

Cart Etiquette

Follow cart rules: Obey "cart path only" or "90-degree rule" signs. Never drive on tee boxes or greens.
Park considerately: Park carts to the side or behind greens, never in front, so following groups can hit.
Share the cart: Take turns driving. The person farthest from the hole should drive to save time.

General Courtesy

Respect the course: Don't throw clubs, slam them into the ground, or damage the course in anger.
Control emotions: Golf is frustrating, but maintain composure. Don't let bad shots affect others' enjoyment.
Be honest: Always count all strokes and call penalties on yourself.
Compliment good shots: Be supportive of playing partners. Acknowledge great shots and good play.
Turn off phones: Keep phones on silent. Only take calls in emergencies, away from other players.
